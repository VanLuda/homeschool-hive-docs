# How Do I Start a Homeschool Group?

Listing a co-op, learning pod or micro-school on Famlo is a guided flow you can finish in one sitting. This guide covers what the flow asks, in the order it asks it, and what you set up afterwards. Anyone with a Famlo account can create a listing, and you become its owner.

## Quick Answer

Open the account menu and choose **List your organization**, then select **Get started**. The flow runs twelve screens across three parts: what kind of organization you are and where you serve families, then your name, description, photos and categories, then your screening questions and a review. Nothing is public until you select **Publish** on the last screen.

## Before you begin

Three answers make the whole flow faster, because they are the first things you are asked.

**Which of the eighteen types you are.** The type shapes the rest of the flow and becomes part of your public address, so a co-op called Riverbend ends up at `famlo.co/co-op/riverbend`.

**Where you serve families.** A listing is either a physical location or an online one. It cannot be both.

**Who can join.** Whether you are listed publicly, and whether you review applicants, are two separate questions. You can change either one later.

## Step 1: Start your listing

1. Open the account menu and choose **List your organization**. If you already run one, the same menu offers **Add organization**.
2. Read the opening screen and select **Get started**.

Your progress is saved from the moment you answer the location question. You can close the tab, come back, and pick up where you left off.

## Step 2: Choose your organization type

The first real question is **Which of these best describes your organization?** There are eighteen types, grouped four ways.

| Group | Types |
| --- | --- |
| **Community-led** | Co-op, Learning pod, Community, Meetup, Faith-based community, Support group |
| **Educator-led** | Micro-school, Charter school, Online school, Tutor, Enrichment program, Studio, Sports club, Camp |
| **Place** | Venue, Attraction, Library |
| **Other** | Business |

Each card carries a one-line description, so **Learning pod** reads "Small group with shared curriculum" and **Venue** reads "Space for events & activities". Pick the closest one — the rest of the flow adapts to it, and you can change it later from **Settings**.

## Step 3: Say where you serve families

Two shapes, and you pick one.

- **We have a physical location** — you meet in person, or families can visit you. Start typing your address and pick a suggestion so the map pin lands correctly.
- **We're online** — you operate virtually with no physical address. Then either tick **Available nationwide** or pick the states you serve.

## Step 4: Decide who can join

Three presets. Each sets how you are listed and how people get in, both at once.

| Choice | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | "Listed publicly and anyone can join instantly." Best for meetups, libraries, and attractions. |
| **Closed** | "Listed publicly, but people request to join and you review each one. Members-only events stay private." Best for co-ops, pods, and schools. |
| **Private** | "Hidden from search and directories — reachable only by a link you share, and invite-only." Best for private groups. |

The screen says so itself: "You can change this any time from your organization settings." Settings later splits these into two separate controls, so a publicly listed co-op that still screens every applicant is possible. See [Organization settings](/docs/organizations/settings).

## Step 5: Name it and describe it

**Name** is what families will recognise, up to 100 characters. It needs at least three characters and saves as you type.

Good names say what you are and where you are:

- "Westside Homeschool Co-op"
- "Nature Explorers Field Trip Group"
- "Riverbend Classical Conversations"

**Description** covers what it is, who it is for, and what makes it worth joining, up to 2,000 characters. Write for a family who has never heard of you: your purpose, what you run, how often you meet, and what you expect of members.

## Step 6: Add a cover photo

Drag a photo in, or select the box to choose one. You can add several, and the first one is your cover — it is what people see on your listing and everywhere it appears in the directory.

You can add or replace photos any time afterwards from the **Photos** page in **Settings**, where a listing can hold up to fifteen.

## Step 7: Pick your categories

Categories are the tags families filter the directory by, covering things like academic approach, faith affiliation and subject area. Which groups you are asked about depends on your type, so a library is not asked about educational approach.

**Pick at least one to continue.** This is the one screen in the middle part you cannot skip, and publishing is refused without a category.

## Step 8: Write your screening questions

Up to three free-text questions an applicant answers when they request to join. Each has a **Required** checkbox, and leaving a question blank drops it.

Three is the maximum, and there are no multiple-choice or checkbox questions.

Useful ones:

- "What ages are your children?"
- "How did you hear about us?"
- "Can you commit to attending twice a month?"

This screen only appears for types that run a membership. A venue or a library has nobody to screen, so the flow goes straight to the review.

## Step 9: Review and publish

The review screen summarises **Type**, **Location**, **Who can join**, **Categories** and, where it applies, **Screening questions**. Each row has an **Edit** link that takes you back to that screen and returns you here afterwards.

When it is right, select **Publish**. Your listing goes live and you land on its public page.

:::note The flow never asks for money
There is no membership-pricing screen and no payout screen anywhere in it. You are not asked for a price, a membership fee, or your bank details, and publishing is not held up waiting for any of them. Dues and payouts are both configured later, from **Settings**, and only if you want them.
:::

## After you publish

### Set up dues, if you charge them

Membership dues are a **Settings** block, not part of the flow. Two things have to be true before you can charge: your visibility is **Closed** or **Private**, and your payouts are set up. A fully open listing cannot bill.

Pick one cadence for everybody — **Monthly**, **Every 2 months**, **Quarterly**, **Every 6 months**, **Annually**, or a **One-time fee**. Families do not choose. See [Membership dues](/docs/organizations/membership-dues).

Famlo's fee comes out of your side of a card payment. It is never added to what a family pays, and there is no setting to pass it on, because there is nothing to pass on. [What's free and what's paid](/docs/families/whats-free-and-whats-paid) sets out what you pay and when.

### Invite your first members

There are no invite links and no invite codes. You invite an **email address**.

- If that address already has a Famlo account, the person is added and emailed, and the email names their role.
- If it does not, they get an invitation email. Signing up with that same address joins them automatically.

**Invite members** is owner-only, and takes a CSV of up to 500 rows with two columns: `email` and `role`. See [Members, roles, and messaging](/docs/organizations/members-and-roles).

### Schedule your first activity

An organization with nothing on the calendar gives families nothing to say yes to. Put one date up before you start recruiting. See [Scheduling activities](/docs/organizations/activities).

### Build a leadership team

There are six roles, each a fixed bundle of permissions. You cannot build a new role or edit an existing one — custom roles do not exist on Famlo.

| Role | What it can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Full control: settings, roles, activities, members and billing |
| **Co-Leader** | Edit the listing, run activities, approve members |
| **Event Manager** | Create and run activities and registrations |
| **Member Manager** | Review and approve people joining |
| **Billing Manager** | Manage payments and billing, and view payouts |
| **Member** | View the organization |

Only the owner can change roles or invite anyone. Two consequences catch people out: a **Co-Leader** cannot change roles or open billing, and a **Member Manager** is not an administrator — approving join requests is the only thing it adds.

Separately from a role, you can give one person access to messages about specific activities. That is how a parent who teaches one class gets their families' questions without being promoted. See [Members, roles, and messaging](/docs/organizations/members-and-roles).

## Tips

- **Start small.** A few well-run dates beat a semester you cannot staff. You can add more whenever you like.
- **Set expectations in writing.** **Join rules** in **Settings** is a free-text box every applicant can be made to accept before joining.
- **Recruit a co-leader early.** Running a co-op alone is how co-ops quietly stop.
- **Announce per activity.** Every announcement belongs to one activity and goes to the people connected to it. There is no organization-wide broadcast and no newsletter, so plan your communication around the activities themselves.
- **Publish before it is perfect.** The name, description, photos and categories are all editable afterwards, and a listing nobody can find recruits nobody.

## Troubleshooting

**I can't get past the categories screen.** Pick at least one tag. The screen says "Pick at least one to continue," and publishing is refused without one.

**Publish came back with "Not quite ready to publish".** Three things are checked at that moment: a real name of at least three characters, a filled-in location, and at least one category. The reasons are listed above the summary.

**I closed the tab and lost my work.** You did not. Open **List your organization** again and select **Continue** on the draft under **In progress**.

**I picked the wrong type.** On the review screen, select **Edit** beside **Type**. After publishing, change **Type** on the **Settings** page.

**Saving dues says my visibility must be Closed or Hidden.** Go to **Membership & joining** in **Settings**, choose **Closed** or **Private**, save, then come back to dues.

**I can't find where to set a membership price during setup.** There isn't one. Dues live in **Settings** after you publish.

**I can't find where to create a custom role.** There is no such thing. There are six fixed roles, set on the **Members** page one person at a time.

**My listing does not appear in the directory.** Check that **Published (visible in the directory)** is ticked on the **Settings** page. A listing set to **Private** visibility is deliberately not listed anywhere.
