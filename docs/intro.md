# Welcome to Famlo

Famlo connects families with the co-ops, classes, field trips, and meetups near
them — and gives the people running those a place to organize them.

Which half of the help center you want depends on which side you are on. Most
people only ever need one.

## If you are a family

Famlo is free for you, always. You pay the price an organizer sets, and nothing
else, to anyone.

- **Find activities near you** — search by what your children are interested in,
  their ages, and whether it meets in person or online
- **Register in one place** — one family profile covers every signup, so you are
  not typing the same allergy information into a fifth form
- **Keep track of it all** — everything you have booked in one calendar, which
  you can subscribe to from the calendar app you already use
- **Talk to organizers** — one inbox for every conversation, with the activity
  right beside it

**Start here:** [Getting started as a new family](/docs/families/getting-started),
then [What's free and what's paid](/docs/families/whats-free-and-whats-paid) if
you want the money question answered before anything else.

## If you run an organization

A co-op, a class, a tutoring practice, a library programme, a microschool — all
of it works the same way. Listing is free, and you pay only when you collect a
card payment.

- **Get found** — your listing appears on the map to families searching nearby
- **Schedule activities** — one-off or repeating, with tickets, capacity, and who
  can see them
- **Run the day** — rosters, attendance, and a record of who dropped a child off
  and who collected them
- **Handle the money** — card checkout through your own payment account, dues
  billed on a schedule, and scholarship funds that carry no fee at all

**Start here:** [Listing your organization](/docs/organizations/listing-your-organization),
then [Getting paid](/docs/organizations/getting-paid).

## If something here is wrong

Every page has a **Was this page helpful?** control at the bottom. Answering No
opens a box where you can say what you were actually looking for, and that is
what decides which pages get fixed first.

For anything about your own account, [open a support ticket](/docs/account/support)
instead — that reaches a person.
